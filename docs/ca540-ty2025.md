# California Form 540 calculation workpaper — TY2025

OpenTax supports a narrow **California TY2025 Form 540 calculation workpaper**, not a complete state return, tax-preparation service, filing-readiness review, or electronic filing path. It does not prepare the federal return. Do not use it for TY2022–2024, Form 540 2EZ, Form 540NR, amended returns, or any unsupported California form or situation.

## What it calculates

For full-year California residents with reviewed inputs, the calculation exposes these Form 540 lines:

- Lines 12–19: state wages; federal AGI adjusted by Schedule CA Part I line 27 columns B and C; regular standard deduction; taxable income.
- Lines 31–35: regular tax using the FTB TY2025 tax table through $100,000 taxable income and rate schedules above $100,000; exemption credit and its tax limitation; total tax.
- Lines 40, 45–48, 61–64: supplied special/other credits and taxes, and computed total tax.
- Lines 95, 97, 100: supplied reviewed payments-after-use-tax-and-penalty and computed overpayment/refund or balance due.

The worksheet requires a single validated `ca540` input record. Monetary inputs are whole dollars. Schedule CA column totals must be prepared from the official Schedule CA; federal amounts must not be copied into California columns without reconciliation. Fields for special taxes, credits, and payments are supplied amounts, not calculations of the underlying schedules. The app does not determine whether those amounts are eligible or complete. Review flags and calculation blockers are not substitutes for professional review.

Example workflow with synthetic facts only:

```sh
opentax return create --year 2025 --form ca540
opentax form add --returnId RETURN_ID --node_type ca540 '{"taxYear":2025,"residency":"full_year_ca","filingStatus":"single","federalAgi":75000,"stateWages":81000,"scheduleCaSubtractions":1000,"scheduleCaAdditions":200,"usesItemizedDeductions":false,"personalExemptions":1,"blindExemptions":0,"seniorExemptions":0,"dependentExemptions":0,"taxpayerCanBeClaimedAsDependent":false,"line34SpecialTax":0,"line40ChildDependentCareCredit":0,"line45SpecialCredits":0,"line46RenterCredit":0,"line61AlternativeMinimumTax":0,"line62BehavioralHealthServicesTax":0,"line63OtherTaxes":0,"line95PaymentsAfterUseTaxAndPenalty":4000,"review":{"fullYearCaliforniaResidence":true,"filingStatusEligibility":true,"federalAndCaliforniaIncomeReconciled":true,"standardDeductionEligibility":true,"creditsAndTaxesReviewed":true,"paymentsUseTaxAndPenaltyReviewed":true}}'
opentax return get --returnId RETURN_ID
opentax return validate --returnId RETURN_ID --format json
opentax return validate --returnId RETURN_ID --format text
opentax return export --returnId RETURN_ID --type pdf
```

The example is synthetic. Its zero-valued taxes/credits and review flags do not establish that a taxpayer has no such amounts; only enter values after checking official schedules.

The PDF is a locally generated preparation worksheet. It is **not an official Form 540, not e-fileable, and not a filing-readiness determination**. MeF export is rejected. Review the official forms, supporting schedules, residency and filing-status rules, payment requirements, health coverage penalty, use tax, and signatures yourself. File and pay through FTB using its approved channels; OpenTax does not infer or authorize submission or payment.

## Explicit coverage and readiness diagnostics

Validation inspects the loaded return snapshot without changing it. A successful command exit means an inspection report was produced, not that the return can be filed. JSON retains `canFile: false` and adds `validationScope: "ca540-workpaper"`, the `workpaper` profile, typed `readiness`, `gates`, `coverage`, `documents`, and `externalChecks`.

For a schema-valid synthetic workpaper whose review answers are all affirmative:

| Gate | Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scope` | `unsupported` | Complete return preparation remains unsupported. |
| `inputs` | `passed` | Exactly one workpaper input satisfies its schema. |
| `calculation` | `passed` | Selected arithmetic completed, not full-return verification. |
| `review` | `passed` | Required workpaper review answers are affirmative, not independently proven facts. |
| `required_documents` | `not_run` | Aggregate inputs cannot establish all required supporting documents. |
| `mef_schema` | `unavailable` | No official FTB XML schema validation has run. |
| `local_rules` | `unavailable` | No official FTB local business-rule evaluation has run. |

The local-readiness result is `blocked`. `CA540_SCOPE_NOT_FILING_READY` remains, alongside explicit missing-document/schema/rule limitations. Review findings use stable semantic IDs rather than numbered `CA540_REVIEW_n` positions. Missing or malformed input yields structured input findings and no calculated values; get/PDF refuse unavailable calculations rather than inventing zeros.

Coverage lists each printed-field/line and accepted-schedule requirement with its official source, implementation/evidence references, and separate **capability** (`computed`, `supplied_input`, `unsupported`, `unavailable`) and **applicability** (`required`, `not_applicable`, `unknown`). Missing applicability facts remain unknown. Zero aggregate amounts and affirmative review answers do not prove that a schedule is unnecessary. Scenario references identify evidence assertions, not complete-return or certification claims. No filing-ready percentage is calculated.

FTB-record checks, provider qualification, and transmission/acceptance remain separately `unverified`. The public FTB version table is not an installed schema package; official artifacts require authorized access. No flag, including `--force` or `--draft`, enables California MeF export.

The worksheet is paginated so coverage and limitations remain visible; every page retains its unofficial/non-e-fileable warning. It reports supplied amounts as inputs, not as prepared underlying schedules.

## Calculation correction and evidence

The exemption-credit phaseout follows the line 32 AGI Limitation Worksheet: personal/blind/senior credits and dependent credits are reduced and floored separately before addition. For the source-derived synthetic regression (single, federal AGI $327,203, one personal exemption and one dependent), line 32 is $295, not the prior $268.

Canonical cases live under `benchmark/cases/ca540/2025/`. The two line-31 cases remain single-line evidence; baseline and mixed-exemption workpapers assert selected amounts plus intentional non-readiness. No complete-return case is supported. `bun run test:ca540` executes CA tests explicitly; `check:cases` and `check:oracle` enforce CA source and typed expectation evidence. `bun run bench --form ca540 --year 2025 --json` runs the canonical CA comparisons with exact whole-dollar results, not the federal benchmark's legacy tolerance.

## Material exclusions and blockers

- Part-year and nonresident cases, including 540NR allocation, are not calculated; they receive blockers.
- Itemized deductions and the dependent standard-deduction worksheet are not calculated; review/eligibility blockers prevent a filing-ready result.
- Schedule CA is not prepared. Federal AGI and state wages are inputs; the user reconciles federal income with California-specific additions/subtractions using Schedule CA.
- Special taxes, AMT, behavioral health services tax, other taxes, credits, withholding, estimates, use tax, and health coverage penalty schedules are not computed. Relevant amounts must come from separately reviewed source schedules; required review answers cannot be omitted.
- Not all Form 540 lines, supporting schedules, eligibility rules, phaseouts, carryovers, or FTB validations are implemented. Validation always reports that the tool is not filing-ready.
- No TY2022–2024 calculations, 540 2EZ, 540NR, amended returns, official-form rendering, MeF, e-filing, submission, or payment support.

## Primary FTB sources

- [2025 Form 540](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540.pdf): line layout and required signature.
- [2025 Instructions for Form 540](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-instructions.html): lines 12–19 (state AGI and deduction), line 31 (tax table/rate schedules), line 32 (exemption-credit limitation), line 64 (total tax), line 95 and settlement lines. FTB notes that its instructions summarize tax law and are not authoritative law.
- [2025 Schedule CA (540)](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-ca.pdf) and [instructions](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-ca-instructions.html): California additions/subtractions and itemized-deduction adjustments; federal and California law do not always conform.
- [2025 California Tax Table](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-taxtable.pdf) and [2025 Tax Rate Schedules](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-tax-rate-schedules.pdf): independent line 31 values and taxable-income thresholds.
- [FTB October 2025 Tax News](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/about-ftb/newsroom/tax-news/2025/10.html): TY2025 standard deduction, exemption-credit amounts, and tax-rate schedule publication.

These references support the implemented arithmetic only. They do not establish that a user's facts, supporting forms, or resulting return are complete or legally correct.
